* Show the Lunarfall footer version inline after SMF credits with a # separator, directly from local theme_info.xml and independently of SMF's stored theme version; read once per request, reject unsafe/invalid metadata and preserve footer links.
* Remove popup border, shadow, rounding and surplus spacing from desktop Themes and Layout and Logs administration tabs; preserve tab colors and active states.
* Improve administration code-block readability with a dark background, light text, clearer headers and larger monospace text; preserve code selection and expansion controls.
* Hide generic mobile-menu launchers on desktop while retaining mobile controls and the desktop menu/tab content.
* Remove the administration home Consider Helping donation block and its dedicated styles.
* Show profile popup shortcuts as icons with visible SMF labels in two columns, without separator dashes or link underlines; retain URLs and accessible names, remove CSS tooltip bubbles, and prevent mobile overflow.
* Use a dark Lunarfall palette, readable text, underlined links and visible keyboard focus for empty unread-topic/reply notices.
